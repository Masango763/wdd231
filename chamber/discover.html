Discover Harare

The commercial engine and innovation capital of Zimbabwe.

Key Statistics

  • Population: ~2.1 Million (Metro)
  • Elevation: 1,480 meters (4,860 ft)
  • Primary Sectors: Finance, Tech, Mining, Agriculture
  • Established: 1890 (as Fort Salisbury)

Visitor Information

Harare features a subtropical highland climate with pleasant warm summers and mild winters. Robert Gabriel Mugabe International Airport links the city globally.

Samora Machel Avenue

Home to major corporate headquarters, banking institutions, and the Harare Regional Chamber of Commerce.

Harare Gardens

A tranquil oasis featuring lush lawns, exotic trees, and regular musical and cultural festivals.

National Gallery of Zimbabwe

Exhibiting world-renowned Shona stone sculpture collections and contemporary African art.

Mukuvisi Woodlands

An urban wildlife sanctuary offering walking trails, zebras, giraffes, and birdwatching.
