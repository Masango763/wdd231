AgriPulse Zimbabwe

Agricultural Information and Farming Resources Website

1. Site Name

AgriPulse Zimbabwe combines “Agri”, representing agriculture, with “Pulse”, representing current information, updates and insights. The name reflects the purpose of the website, which is to provide accessible agricultural information, farming resources, crop information and seasonal insights for people interested in agriculture in Zimbabwe[cite: 3].

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of AgriPulse Zimbabwe is to provide accessible agricultural information and educational resources for smallholder farmers, agricultural students and people who are interested in farming in Zimbabwe. The website will provide information about common crops grown in Zimbabwe, seasonal farming practices, weather and crop insights, sustainable farming, crop management and soil conservation. It will also provide useful agricultural resources and allow visitors to submit questions or inquiries through a contact form[cite: 3].

3. Scenarios

  • Where can I find information about crops commonly grown in Zimbabwe and basic guidance on how to care for them?[cite: 3]
  • Where can I find information about seasonal farming and weather conditions that may affect my crops?[cite: 3]
  • Where can I find useful resources about sustainable farming, crop management and soil conservation?[cite: 3]
  • How can I submit a question or inquiry about agriculture and farming?[cite: 3]

4. Colour Schema

The website will use a natural agricultural colour palette[cite: 3]

Colour Hex Code Application Meaning / Purpose
Dark Green #2E5D34 Header, navigation, main headings, footer[cite: 3] Agriculture, growth and nature[cite: 3]
Light Green #A7C957 Buttons, highlights, cards, accents[cite: 3] Freshness and visual emphasis[cite: 3]
Cream #F7F3E9 Main page background and content sections[cite: 3] Clean, comfortable reading area[cite: 3]
Dark Grey #333333 Body text[cite: 3] Readable text and strong contrast[cite: 3]

Colour Schema Application: Dark green will be used for the header, navigation, major headings and footer. Light green will be used for buttons, highlighted information, cards and accents. Cream will provide the main background. Dark grey will be used for body text[cite: 3].

5. Colour Schema Use in the Site Plan Document

This site plan uses the selected AgriPulse Zimbabwe colour schema. Dark green is used for major headings and titles, light green is used for secondary emphasis and a light cream background is represented in the colour schema. Dark grey is used for body text. These colours will also be used consistently on the final website[cite: 3].

6. Typography

Primary Font: Poppins
Poppins will be used for website headings, navigation, buttons and important labels. Its clean appearance will help make headings easy to identify and give the website a modern appearance[cite: 3].

Secondary Font: Arial
Arial will be used for paragraphs, descriptions, scenario questions and other body content because it is simple and easy to read[cite: 3].
